Editor sign-in

Your writing desk.

Write, add your photos, keep drafts, and publish to {{ site.site_name }}. You sign in with your email; there is no password to remember.

Open my writing desk Opens in the writing app we use (Pages CMS). Bookmark this page, smartselectlabs.com/admin, and start here every time.

  1. Sign in with your email

    Press Open my writing desk, choose Continue with email, and type the Gmail address Lewis set up for you.

  2. Type the 6-digit code

    Check your Gmail for “Your Pages CMS temporary code” and type the six numbers. You stay signed in for a while after that.

  3. Pick a kind of post

    On the left, choose the kind of post, then press Add an entry. The outline is already there: replace the sample lines with your own words.

  4. Add a photo, save often

    Use Upload under Main picture for your own photo. Leave Keep as a draft ON while you write and press Save as often as you like.

  5. Publish

    When it is ready, turn Keep as a draft OFF and press Save. Your post appears on the site in about two minutes.

Good to know

  • It works on your phone too. Same steps.
  • To fix a post that is already live, open it from the list, change the words, press Save.
  • Your own photos only. Never save a picture from Amazon or a store website.
  • Don’t type exact Amazon prices (“about $90” is fine). Amazon’s rule.
  • A weekly rhythm works best: one post a week, the same day each week.
  • Stuck? Message Lewis. Nothing you do here can break the website.
