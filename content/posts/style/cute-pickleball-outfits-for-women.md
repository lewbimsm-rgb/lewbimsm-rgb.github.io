Last Tuesday I stood at my closet at seven in the morning holding a cotton T-shirt and old running shorts, and thought, it's the park, who is looking. By the second game the shirt was soaked and clinging, the shorts had crept up on every lunge, and I sat through coffee afterward damp and pretending I was fine. So here is what I have worked out about cute pickleball outfits since, which is mostly about fabric and a liner, and only a little about cute.

```quick
**The short version:** pick one of five outfit formulas, in a fabric that wicks sweat and stretches four ways, with a pocket that holds a ball. A tennis dress with built-in shorts is the easiest place to start. If I could point you to one piece it would be the **lululemon Align Dress**; if the price makes you wince, the **Vuori Halo Performance Skirt** with a tank you already own gets you most of the way there.
```

## The five formulas behind cute pickleball outfits

**A tennis dress with built-in shorts** is the one-piece answer. The liner lets you lunge without a thought, most hide a ball pocket, and the lululemon Align Dress is the softest I know of.

**A skort and a tank** is what most of the women I play with wear, because one good skort goes with every tank you already own. The two skorts I would look at are below.

**High-rise shorts and a cropped tee**, for those of us who never took to skirts. The lululemon Hotty Hot High-Rise Lined Short comes in 2.5-inch and 4-inch inseams; take the 4-inch, with a tee that stops at the waistband.

**Leggings and a thin long sleeve**, for the 55-degree morning when a dress feels like a dare. Align leggings are the soft choice, and the Vuori Daydream Crew keeps the sun off your forearms until it ends up tied around your waist.

**One light layer over any of those** for early open play, zippered so it comes off in one motion between games, which a hoodie wrestled over a visor never does.

## What I read on the tag

Three things: moisture-wicking, four-way stretch and a UPF number. Wicking pulls sweat to the surface so it dries, stretch lets you bend without the hem pulling, and UPF 50 lets through about 2 percent of the sun's UV, which the [Skin Cancer Foundation](https://www.skincancer.org/prevention/sun-protection/clothing/) calls excellent. Cotton fails all three, which brings us back to Tuesday.

Then the court-to-coffee rule, which is the whole difference between cute pickleball outfits and gym clothes: if I would not wear it into a café afterward, I do not buy it. That rules out neon that only works on a pro, anything I keep tugging at, and leggings that go see-through when you squat in the store mirror.

Two fit notes, at our age: a 4-inch liner stops the chafing on a humid day, and a high-rise waistband sits above the hip bones and does not roll, which I care about more than I used to. A UPF sleeve beats sunscreen on the arms because you cannot forget to reapply it; the [pickleball skincare](/pickleball-skincare/) post covers the rest.

## Common questions

```faq
Q: What should a woman wear to play pickleball for the first time?
A: Whatever athletic clothes you already own, as long as the fabric is not cotton and the shoes are court shoes. A skort or shorts, a wicking tank and a visor is the standard outfit at most courts. Save the cute pickleball outfits for after a few sessions, once you know you will keep playing.

Q: Do I need a skirt with built-in shorts?
A: Yes, or shorts under the skirt. You bend, lunge and squat for low balls at the kitchen line, and the liner keeps you covered. A 2.5-inch liner is standard; a 4-inch liner stops chafing on humid days and suits a lot of the women I play with better.

Q: Is UPF clothing worth it for pickleball?
A: If you play outdoors for more than an hour at a time, yes. A UPF 50 fabric lets about 2 percent of UV through, and unlike sunscreen you cannot forget to reapply it. Pair it with SPF 30 or higher on your face, neck and hands.
```
