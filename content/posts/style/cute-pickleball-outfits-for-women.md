## The five formulas behind cute pickleball outfits

Every one of the cute pickleball outfits we see at open play fits one of these five formulas. Pick the one that matches your shape and the weather, then buy one good version of it.

**1. Tennis dress with built-in shorts.** The one-piece answer. The liner keeps you covered on a lunge, and many tennis dresses hide a ball pocket in the liner or under the hem. The lululemon Align Dress is the softest version we know of. Add court shoes and a visor, and that is the whole outfit.

**2. Skort and tank.** The most common outfit at the courts for a reason: one good skort works with every tank you own. The Vuori Halo Performance Skirt has liner shorts and ball pockets. The Athleta A Game Skort adds UPF 50+ fabric. The NikeCourt Advantage Pleated Skirt is the classic tennis look with built-in shorts.

**3. High-rise shorts and a cropped tee.** For women who never liked skirts. The lululemon Hotty Hot High-Rise Lined Short comes in 2.5-inch and 4-inch inseams; pick the 4-inch for more coverage. Vuori's Halo Performance Short is the softer cousin. Pair either with a boxy cropped tee that ends at the waistband, not above it.

### Two formulas for cool mornings and early open play

**4. Leggings and a long sleeve for cool mornings.** On a 55-degree morning, full-length leggings and a thin long sleeve beat a dress with a jacket. lululemon Align leggings are the soft choice. The Vuori Daydream Crew is a light long sleeve that wicks sweat and covers your arms from the sun. It ties around your waist by the second game.

**5. A light layer over anything for early open play.** Add one layer to any of the four above: a quarter-zip, a thin crew or a windbreaker. The layer should come off in one motion between games. Zippers help; a hoodie you have to wrestle over a visor does not.

## What matters when you choose

**Fabric.** Look for three things on the tag: moisture-wicking, four-way stretch and a UPF rating. Wicking fabric pulls sweat to the surface so it dries, and stretch lets you lunge without the hem pulling. UPF 50 means the fabric lets through about 2 percent of the sun's UV, or one fiftieth. The [Skin Cancer Foundation](https://www.skincancer.org/prevention/sun-protection/clothing/) rates that as excellent, and UPF 30 to 49 as very good. Cotton fails all three tests, so save the cotton tee for the drive home.

**Pockets.** You need one pocket that holds a ball without bouncing it against your thigh. Liner pockets, hem pockets and a zip pocket on a skort all work. A phone pocket is a bonus, not a requirement.

**A visor or cap.** A visor keeps the sun off your face and lets heat out the top of your head. A cap also covers the part in your hair, which burns faster than you think. Either one finishes the outfit, and neither costs much.

**The court-to-coffee rule.** If you would not wear it to a café afterward, do not buy it. That rules out see-through leggings, neon that only works on a pro and anything you keep tugging at. That is the whole difference between cute pickleball outfits and gym clothes. It is also why a tennis dress wins so often: put a cardigan over it and it reads as a dress.

## Sizing notes for women over 50

**Liner length.** Built-in shorts come in roughly 2.5-inch and 4-inch inseams. The longer liner stops chafing on a humid day and stays put when you bend for a low ball.

**Waist.** A high-rise waistband sits above the hip bones and does not roll when you move. The A Game skort is high rise, and lululemon labels its Hotty Hot shorts by rise, so you can choose.

**Support.** If you need real support in a dress, look for a built-in shelf bra or a cup-sized version, and read the product page for the support it offers. Otherwise wear your own sports bra under it and pick straps that cover it.

**Length.** Product pages list the skirt length. A longer cut covers more when you bend and still looks like a skort, so read that number before the color. Athleta's Ace Tennis Skort runs up to 3X, which matters when other charts stop at a size 12.

**Sun.** Long sleeves in a UPF fabric beat sunscreen on the forearms for one reason. You cannot forget to reapply a sleeve. Our [pickleball skincare](/pickleball-skincare/) guide covers the rest.

## What to skip

**Cotton anything.** It soaks up sweat, stays wet, chafes and dries slowly. This includes the cute cotton tee from the gift shop.

**Leggings without the squat test.** Thin leggings turn see-through in sunlight. Squat in front of a mirror in the store before you buy.

**A skirt with no liner.** You will be bending for low balls all morning. No liner, no skirt.

**A hoodie as the layer.** It traps heat, it will not come off over a visor, and it ends up on the fence.

**Running shoes.** The cutest outfit on the court does not fix running shoes rolling your ankle. Read our guide to the [best pickleball shoes for women](/best-pickleball-shoes-for-women/) before you spend on clothes.

## Common questions

```faq
Q: What should a woman wear to play pickleball for the first time?
A: Any athletic clothes you own, as long as the fabric is not cotton and your shoes are court shoes. A skort or shorts, a wicking tank and a visor is the standard outfit. Buy the cute pickleball outfits after a few sessions, once you know you will keep playing.
Q: Do I need a skirt with built-in shorts?
A: Yes, or shorts under the skirt. You bend, lunge and squat for low balls at the kitchen line, and a liner keeps you covered. A 2.5-inch liner is standard. A 4-inch liner stops chafing on humid days and suits many women over 50 better.
Q: Is UPF clothing worth it for pickleball?
A: If you play outdoors for more than an hour at a time, yes. A UPF 50 fabric lets about 2 percent of UV through, and unlike sunscreen you cannot forget to reapply it. Pair it with SPF 30 or higher on your face, neck and hands.
Q: Can I wear leggings to play pickleball?
A: Yes, and on cool mornings they beat a dress. Pick a full-length, high-rise pair with a wide waistband and a side pocket. Do the squat test in a mirror before you buy, because thin leggings go see-through in sunlight. Add a thin long sleeve and you have formula four.
```
