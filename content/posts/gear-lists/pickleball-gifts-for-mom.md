The best pickleball gifts for mom are the small things she uses up and never buys for herself: fresh overgrips, a sleeve of outdoor balls, a hat with a real brim. If you want one bigger present, make it a lesson or a forgiving paddle under a hundred dollars. Leave the $250 one on the shelf. I am a mom who plays, so consider this a list from the receiving end.

```quick
**The short answer:** for a stocking, overgrips or three outdoor balls. For a wrapped present, a brimmed hat, sun sleeves or a sling bag with a fence hook. The gift she will talk about is a lesson. And if you want to give a paddle, choose a 16 mm one from a real brand's starter line, which runs about $80 to $100.
```

## The small ones she will use up

Overgrips are the thin wraps that go over a paddle handle, and they wear out the way socks do. When one goes slick she grips harder, and a hard grip is where sore elbows start. A fresh three-pack is a kinder present than it looks. Tourna Grip feels dry and a little chalky, and Wilson Pro feels tacky. If you do not know which she likes, buy the white Wilson. Everybody can live with it.

Balls are the other thing a new player never has enough of. Outdoor balls are hard, with forty small holes, and they crack on cold mornings. The Franklin X-40 is the ball at most public courts. Give her three, and she is practicing with what everyone else hits. If she plays in a gym, ask first, since indoor balls are softer and a different thing altogether.

## Pickleball gifts for mom that get wrapped

For about $25 to $40 you can fix one of the small annoyances of a morning at the courts. A hat with a full brim covers her ears and the back of her neck, which a visor does not. Sun sleeves, the UPF 50 kind, mean one less place to rub in sunscreen. They also feel cooler than bare skin once they are damp. A sling bag with a fence hook keeps her things off a wet court and at eye level. An insulated bottle she can open with one hand is the fourth idea. ## The gift that is not a thing

An hour with a coach is the present most of us would never buy for ourselves. It feels like a lot of fuss for a game we play at the park. It is also the one that changes the most, because in one morning a good teacher can fix a grip or a serve she would otherwise keep for a year. Call the recreation center or club where she plays. Ask whether they sell a lesson or a beginner clinic as a gift. Many will write up a certificate even when the website says nothing about it.

## If you want to give a paddle

Look at three numbers and ignore the rest of the box. The core should be 16 mm thick, because the thicker core is the soft, forgiving one. The weight should land between 7.6 and 8.0 ounces, since her arm pays for every ounce by game three. And the grip should be 4.25 inches around, which fits most women's hands. The Selkirk SLK Evo Control Max meets all three and usually sells for $80 to $100. ## What I would leave in the store

A pro paddle, for one. The $250 models are stiffer and built for people who already know where the ball is going. A beginner mostly learns that they sting. Novelty gifts with a pickle pun on them are fine as a joke. I have a drawer of them, and you are welcome to look. And unless you are sure of her size, skip the outfit, because returning a skort in January is nobody's idea of a present.

```faq
Q: What grip size should I buy?
A: A 4.25 inch grip fits most women. If it turns out a little small, an overgrip builds it up, but a grip that is too big cannot be made smaller.
Q: Is a two-paddle set from Amazon a good gift?
A: It is fine for finding out whether she likes the game. Those paddles are heavier and deader than a real starter paddle, so if she already plays every week, one good paddle is the better present.
Q: Indoor or outdoor balls?
A: Ask where she plays. Outdoor balls are harder with smaller holes, and indoor balls are softer with bigger ones. They do not swap well.
```

Whichever of these pickleball gifts for mom you choose, tuck in a note that says you will come and watch a game. It costs nothing, and it is the one I would pick.
