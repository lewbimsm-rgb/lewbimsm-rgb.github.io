Privacy Policy

What information smartselectlabs.com collects, why, and the choices you have.

Effective date: October 5, 2026

SmartSelect Labs (“we”, “us”) runs the website smartselectlabs.com. This page explains, in plain English, what information the site collects, why, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, email us at hello@smartselectlabs.com.

What we collect

  • Contact messages. If you write to us, we receive the name, email address and message you send. We use them only to reply to you.
  • Usage statistics. Like most websites we may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to see which pages are read, roughly where visitors come from, and what devices they use. This information is aggregated and does not identify you by name.

We do not have user accounts, and we do not sell personal information.

Cookies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. We use them for analytics (above) and through the affiliate and advertising partners described below. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work.

Affiliate links

Many product links on this site are affiliate links (see our affiliate disclosure). When you click one, the retailer, for example Amazon, Selkirk Sport or Target, may set a cookie so it can credit us if you make a purchase. Those retailers have their own privacy policies; Amazon’s is at amazon.com/privacy.

Advertising

We may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Google explains how it uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, time) for security and troubleshooting. GitHub’s privacy statement is at docs.github.com/site-policy.

How long we keep information

Contact messages are kept as long as needed to deal with your question. Analytics data is kept in aggregated form.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@smartselectlabs.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is written for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
