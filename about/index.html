About SmartSelect Labs

A small, family-run pickleball blog for people who play at the park, the rec center or the community court.

SmartSelect Labs is a small, family-run blog about getting into pickleball and choosing gear without the hype.

We started it because most pickleball buying advice is written for tournament players, or is really just an advertisement. We write for the rest of us: people who play at the park, the rec center or the community court, who want to pick a paddle that suits them, protect their knees, and have more fun.

What you’ll find here

  • Beginner guides. How the game works, what to bring to your first open play, how scoring and the kitchen rule actually work, and the handful of habits that make new players better fast.
  • Gear reviews. Paddles, court shoes, balls and bags compared on what matters: comfort, control, durability and price. We say who each product is for, and who should skip it.
  • Health and recovery. Staying on the court: warm-ups that take two minutes, protecting knees and elbows, and playing well into your sixties and seventies.

How we work

When we recommend something, we explain who it is for and why. When we have used a product ourselves, we say so. When a recommendation is based on research and other players’ experience rather than hands-on use, we say that too.
